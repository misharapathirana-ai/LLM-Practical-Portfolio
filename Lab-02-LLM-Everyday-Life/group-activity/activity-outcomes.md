# Activity outcomes

**Problem:** Group 7 - The Overloaded Student. Students carry too many items every day and want to carry, organise or reduce them.

**Product:** LightLoad, an app (with an optional colour-coded pouch kit) that reads a student's timetable and tells them what to bring today, what to leave behind and how heavy their bag will be.

**Target user:** university students who commute and carry a laptop daily, especially first and second years.

**Key features:** today's pack, leave-it-behind suggestions, bag weight meter, last check before leaving, weather adjustments, optional pouch kit.

**Visual prototype:** [screenshots/lightload_screens.svg](../screenshots/lightload_screens.svg)

**Decision:** option B (app plus optional pouch kit) was chosen over a smart backpack and a locker-share network. Full details are in the lab README.
