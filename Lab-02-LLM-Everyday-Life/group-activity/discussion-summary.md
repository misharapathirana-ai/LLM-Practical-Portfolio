# Discussion summary

There was no group discussion because I worked individually. The discussion happened between me and the LLM.

- **Options compared:** a smart backpack (impressive but needs hardware and is costly), an app with an optional pouch kit (cheap to build and test, depends on timetable input), and a locker-share network (removes weight but needs university infrastructure).
- **Why LightLoad:** it can be built and tested with little cost, and the LLM does real work in it by turning a timetable, assignments and weather into a packing decision with reasons.
- **Limitations raised:** entering a timetable is friction, the LLM cannot know everything a class needs, item weights are estimates, a timetable is personal data, and the concept helps over-packers most.
- **Not done:** the concept was not tested with real students, so its assumptions are unchecked.
